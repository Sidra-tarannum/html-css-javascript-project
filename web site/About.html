Our Story

ISL Pharmacy College (ISLPC) is the flagship program of AMES (The Ahmed Memorial Educational Society), representing the next chapter in our commitment to excellence in professional education.

Building upon the success of ISL Engineering College, established in 2008, ISLPC embodies the vision of providing world-class pharmaceutical education that meets international standards while remaining rooted in our values of integrity, innovation, and service.

Located strategically in Hyderabad, the pharmaceutical capital of India, our college is positioned to leverage the city's rich ecosystem of pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, and healthcare organizations.

AMES Legacy

Irrespective of region, religion, and caste, the society has ever been engaged in imparting quality education. ISL Engineering College, established in 2008, stands as a testament to our commitment to excellence in professional education.

Chocolate cake

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla essence.
  4. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  5. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
  6. Let the cake cool and serve.
